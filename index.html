Consultation outcome

An SBAR is generated for all outcomes — required for IP governance regardless of outcome. Scotland: NHS Pharmacy First Plus prescribing requires GP10 pad and SBAR sent to GP practice.

🏴 NHS Scotland — Pharmacy First Plus: Prescriptions under PFP+ must be written on your NHS Board-issued GP10 pad. Submit a UCF Supply claim via PharmOutcomes / your claim system. SBAR required for GP practice records. Claim code: NHS Pharmacy First Plus Supply. Activity pot payment applies from November 2025 (PCA(P)(2025)24).

Clinical judgement assistant

AI-powered

Ask anything — prescribing decisions, scope of practice, referral justification, drug interactions, off-label use, evidence appraisal. Uses your consultation context plus current UK clinical evidence.

⚠ Internet required — use the downloaded file: The AI assistant calls the Anthropic API directly from your browser. This works when you open the downloaded IP_Consultation_Tool_v2.html file locally. It will not work inside the Claude.ai preview panel due to browser security restrictions on that page. Download the file and open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
📚 Example questions:
• "Is it within my IP scope to prescribe ciprofloxacin/dexamethasone ear drops with a perforated tympanic membrane?"
• "What NICE NG12 criteria apply to this presentation?"
• "Is there a drug interaction between [X] and [Y] in this patient?"
• "Should I refer to GP or manage in community — justify the decision?"
• "What is the evidence for [drug] in [condition] in a patient with [PMH]?"
